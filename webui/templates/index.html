2-of-4 Multi-Factor Confirmation

      
Movies require at least 2 factors (TMDB, Runtime ±4m, Primary AI, Secondary AI) before naming. Unverified rips route to finished/<Title> (Unmatched)/ with a 2×2 preview grid.
