Blinky's Auto DVD Ripper — Quick Guide & How-To

      
Movies vs. TV Box Sets vs. Concert DVDs
For Movies, leave TMDB and Min Runtime (45m) enabled. For TV Series or Music DVDs, uncheck Min Runtime in Setup and switch Database to TVmaze or MusicBrainz (neither requires an API key).

      
On-Demand Local AI Vision
Click Install Local AI (moondream) in Setup to download a compact ~1.7 GB vision model. It stays completely stopped (0 MB RAM) until an unmatched disc needs identification, then spins down immediately.

      
USB Power & Multi-Drive Tips
If using USB optical drives, keep Drive Mode on Sequential Queue and plug into rear motherboard USB 3.0 ports or a powered hub. Internal SATA drives can use Simultaneous mode.
